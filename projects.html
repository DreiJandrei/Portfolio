01Windows desktop app

EmberWatch

A hardware monitor that keeps my PC from overheating mid-game. It shows live CPU, GPU and motherboard temperatures and memory usage, and warns me before anything starts throttling.

Key features
  • Live temperatures and memory usage with 60-second history graphs
  • Overheat alerts with adjustable CPU and GPU limits and a trigger delay, so short spikes don't set them off
  • Optional alert sound and a warning popup on top of the game
  • Always-on-top, click-through gaming overlay with hotkeys, a choice of corner and opacity
  • 17 live temperature and fan sensor readings
  • Can start quietly with Windows in the system tray

Focus: Windows, hardware monitoring, in-game overlay

02Web app for students

StudyBuddy

A free AI study buddy. Students upload their teacher's PDF or PowerPoint and get a reviewer or a practice quiz in about a minute, without signing up.

Key features
  • Upload up to 5 PDF or PowerPoint files at once
  • Reviewer with summarized notes, key terms, flashcards and memory tips
  • Practice quiz with multiple choice, true or false and identification questions in three difficulty levels
  • Instant checking with explanations, retakes with reshuffled questions
  • Print or download results as a PDF

Built with: JavaScript, Google Gemini API, PDF.js, Netlify

04Mobile app

Spotter

A free gym buddy for beginners. Starting out at the gym can be confusing, and trainers and premium fitness apps cost a lot, so I built an app that tells you what to do, shows you how to do it safely and keeps you motivated.

Key features
  • Ready-made Push, Pull, Legs and Full Body routines tailored to your level and goal
  • Form guides with step-by-step instructions and common mistakes for every exercise
  • Quick, no-equipment warm-ups to help prevent injuries
  • Food scan: take a photo of your meal and AI estimates its calories, protein, carbs and fat
  • Workout history and medals for staying consistent
  • Rep Rush mini-game, coins, power-ups and titles you can show on your profile
  • Optional account with cloud backup so progress is never lost

Built with: TypeScript, React Native, Expo, Supabase, Google Gemini API
